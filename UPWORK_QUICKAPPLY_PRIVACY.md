# Privacy Policy for Upwork QuickApply

**Last Updated:** January 2025

## Overview

Upwork QuickApply is a browser extension that helps automate Upwork proposal generation using AI-enhanced cover letters. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Authentication Information
- **Microsoft Account Data**: When you log in, we collect your name and email address from your Microsoft Azure AD account
- **Access Tokens**: Temporary authentication tokens to verify your identity
- **Domain Restriction**: Only users with @pearlthoughts.com email addresses can use this extension

### 2. Job Information
- **Job Identifiers**: We extract job ciphertext (unique job IDs) from Upwork URLs you visit
- **Job Details**: Job titles and descriptions are processed to generate relevant cover letters

### 3. Generated Content
- **Cover Letters**: AI-generated cover letters are temporarily stored locally in your browser
- **Submission Status**: We track whether a cover letter was sent to mark it as used

## How We Use Your Information

- **Authentication**: To verify your identity and restrict access to authorized users
- **Cover Letter Generation**: To create personalized cover letters using AI based on job postings
- **Temporary Storage**: To auto-fill cover letters when you navigate to Upwork application pages
- **Usage Tracking**: To mark cover letters as sent and prevent reuse

## Data Storage

### Local Storage (Your Browser)
- Cover letters are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API
- Data is automatically deleted after 1 hour or when marked as sent
- You can clear this data anytime through your browser settings

### Remote Storage (Our Servers)
- Authentication tokens and user information are stored on our secure servers
- Generated cover letters and job information are stored in our database
- Server location: Railway.app hosting platform

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your data for purposes other than providing the service

We DO share data with:
- **OpenAI**: Job descriptions are sent to OpenAI's API to generate cover letters
- **Microsoft Azure AD**: For authentication purposes only

## Third-Party Services

- **OpenAI API**: Used for AI-powered cover letter generation
- **Microsoft Azure AD**: Used for user authentication
- **Railway.app**: Hosting provider for our backend services

## Permissions Explained

- **activeTab & tabs**: To detect when you're on Upwork job pages
- **storage**: To temporarily save cover letters locally
- **identity**: To authenticate you via Microsoft
- **host_permissions**: To communicate with Upwork.com and our backend API

## Data Retention

- **Local Data**: Automatically deleted after 1 hour or when proposal is sent
- **Server Data**: Retained for service functionality; you can request deletion anytime

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt-out by uninstalling the extension
- Revoke authentication by logging out

## Security

- All API communications use HTTPS encryption
- Authentication tokens are securely stored
- Access is restricted to authorized email domain only

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## Contact

For privacy concerns or data deletion requests, please contact the development team at Pearl Thoughts.

## Compliance

This extension is designed for internal use by Pearl Thoughts employees and complies with applicable data protection regulations.
